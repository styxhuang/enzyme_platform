1. Methodology

The binding free energy ($\Delta G_{bind}$) was calculated using the GB implicit solvent model. The total binding free energy is estimated as:

$\Delta G_{bind} = \Delta H - T\Delta S pprox \Delta E_{MM} + \Delta G_{sol} - T\Delta S$

where:

  • $\Delta E_{MM}$: Gas-phase interaction energy (VDW + Electrostatic)
  • $\Delta G_{sol}$: Solvation free energy (Polar + Non-polar)
  • $T\Delta S$: Entropic contribution (if calculated)
